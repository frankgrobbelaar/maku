A name for Kura's sibling

Kura is 蔵, a storehouse. Its sibling keeps series and films, so these are short Japanese words from the same house: easy to say, two syllables where they can be.

M Maku
My pick

幕 maku · curtain

The curtain that goes up on a show, and an act of a play. Kura and Maku sound like a pair.

No video app by this name turned up

K Koma

コマ koma · a frame

A single frame of film; every series and film is made of them. Short and sharp.

M Mado

窓 mado · window

A screen is a window onto a story. Calm and plain; also a Turkish café chain, far from video.

T Tana

棚 tana · shelf

Kura is the storehouse, Tana its shelf: the closest pair of the six.

Also a well-known notes app

H Hikari

光 hikari · light

Light, as from a projector. Softer, and a syllable longer.

Also Hikari TV, a Japanese streaming service

K Kinema

キネマ kinema · the cinema

The old Japanese word for the pictures. Says what it is at once.
